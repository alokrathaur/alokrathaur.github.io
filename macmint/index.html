Core Capabilities

🧹

Developer & Deep Cache Purge

Target developer caches that silently eat dozens of gigabytes: Xcode DerivedData, CocoaPods, Homebrew, npm, Cargo, and pip caches, plus stale user application crash logs.

💾

System Data Reclaim

Unpack the mysterious "System Data" bar in macOS Storage settings. Safely reclaim sandboxed app group containers, iOS simulator runtimes, and orphaned cache folders.

Complete App Uninstaller

Standard drag-to-trash leaves hundreds of megabytes of preferences, Application Support files, and daemons behind. MacMint tracks every dependency and eliminates them completely.

Startup Items & Daemons Manager

Inspect and disable LaunchAgents, LaunchDaemons, and hidden background login items. Reclaim boot speed and eliminate rogue background processes throttling your CPU.

Explore your entire drive with an interactive radial multi-tier visual map. Drill down into bulky directories and isolate giant files in a matter of seconds.

Directly frees space immediately rather than recycling files into an already overflowing Trash can. Every delete is accompanied by transparent itemized confirmation summaries.
